🌟 Welcome to SkyBlink

Explore how the infrared sky changes over time using NASA's SPHEREx satellite

1

Select a Target

Choose from preset challenge targets — cold brown dwarfs (WISE 0855), high-motion stars (Barnard's Star), or pulsating variables (R Draconis).

2

Play the Blink Flipbook

Press ▶ Play or Spacebar. SkyBlink flips between observation epochs chronologically, revealing motion and variability in the infrared sky.

3

Difference Subtraction

Switch to △ Difference mode to subtract a reference epoch. Static stars vanish — moving objects appear as bright/dark dipoles!

4

Inspect Spectra & Candidates

Click any pixel to see its IR spectrum across SPHEREx's 102 channels. Flagged candidates (>5σ) appear automatically with their sky coordinates.
