How to Explore SPHEREx with SkyBlink

1

Select a Target Object (اختر الجرم الفلكي)

Pick from our Challenge Target chips like WISE 0855 (cold brown dwarf) or Barnard's Star (extreme proper motion).

2

Play the Animated Flipbook (شغّل الأنيميشن)

Press Play Flipbook or Spacebar. The high-speed RAM engine plays observations chronologically up to 24 FPS to reveal parallax and motion.

3

Difference Subtraction & Discovery (طرح الصور)

Switch to Difference mode to subtract a reference epoch from another. Background stars vanish, revealing candidate variable objects and dipoles with >5σ significance.

4

Spectral Inspection (فحص الطيف)

Click on any pixel in the viewer to view its calibrated infrared spectral energy distribution across SPHEREx's 102 spectroscopic channels.
